What fits in a 12 yard skip

When the small ones won't cut it.

Best for

  • Full property clearance — house contents to landfill / charity sort
  • Loft conversion strip-out — joists, plasterboard, insulation
  • Large commercial refurb — office or retail stripped to shell
  • Multi-room renovations — strip-out across whole house

Allowed in the skip

  • Wood, metal, plastic, cardboard
  • Light bulky waste only — no heavy soil or rubble
  • Plasterboard (tell us first so we bag it separately)
  • Most general construction and household waste

Not allowed: fridges & freezers, TVs, tyres, asbestos, batteries, paint, chemicals, anything with fuel.
