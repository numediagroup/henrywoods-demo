If your skip is going on a public road or pavement, yes — the local council issues a permit. We apply for it on your behalf when you book, and include the cost in your total before you pay. On a private drive, no permit is needed.
Fridges & freezers, TVs & monitors, tyres, asbestos, batteries, paint and chemicals, anything containing fuel. Everything else — including plasterboard, soil and rubble — goes in (tell us in advance for the heavy ones so we bring the right lorry).
Standard hire is 14 days. Need longer? Extension is £15 per day. Need it gone sooner? Phone the yard the day before and we'll bring forward the collection at no extra cost.

On concrete, tarmac and block paving, no — we use timber bearers under the feet to spread the weight. On gravel or grass, the marks will be visible while the skip's there. Mention surface in the booking and our driver will plan accordingly.

Every load comes back to the Mill Lane yard and is hand-sorted. Wood, metals, aggregates and plasterboard are separated into recycling streams. We recycle 95% — only the unsortable residue ends up in landfill.
There's a fill line on each skip — load above it and we'll need to remove the excess at the yard, and the driver may refuse collection. Heavy waste (soil, rubble, concrete) is fine in 4/6/8 yard skips but should not be in a 12 yard.
