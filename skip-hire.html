Council permits

If it's going on the road, we sort the permit.

Forgetting the permit is the #1 way skip hire goes wrong. We handle it on your behalf for every south London council we cover.

When do you need a permit? Any skip placed on a public road, pavement or grass verge needs a council-issued permit. Skips on private driveways do not.

  • We apply on your behalf for every booking that needs one — you don't need to phone the council yourself.
  • Cost included before you pay. The permit fee is added to your booking total before you reach the payment screen.
  • Lead time: most councils issue within 3 working days. Order early in the week if you want the skip down by Friday.
  • Reflective markings + lighting are added by our driver as part of the drop-off — required under the Highways Act 1980.
  • Bay suspensions (when the parking bay your skip needs is in use) are also handled by us, charged at council rates.

Councils we apply for permits with: Croydon, Sutton, Merton, Lambeth, Wandsworth. Phone the yard if you're at the edge of these areas.
